About the Championship

Where Developing Programs Meet a National Stage

The National 10s Rugby Championship comes to Bozeman for 2027 — two days of 10s at Bozeman Sports Park, hosted by the Montana Institute of Sport, the nonprofit behind TRY Rugby and the Montana Youth Rugby Union. This is a boys tournament only, bringing together middle school and U16/JV programs from across the country.

Teams from any state are welcome. The championship is built for programs that want national-level competition without the roster depth, travel cost, or logistics of a full 15s tournament.

Ten-a-Side Format

Ten a side on a full pitch: more space, more ball in hand, and more matches across the weekend than a 15s tournament allows.

Two Boys Divisions

Middle School Boys and U16/JV Boys, open to school-based and club programs alike, from any state in the country.

Bozeman, Montana

Hosted in the heart of Big Sky country at Bozeman Sports Park, the same valley that hosts the Great Northwest Challenge each summer.

Why 10s

Ten a Side, More Rugby

  • Fewer players to travel with, which puts a national event in reach for smaller programs.
  • More space, more ball in hand, more touches per player.
  • Shorter matches mean more games across the weekend.
  • A realistic entry point for programs still building depth toward full 15s.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can enter?

Any middle school or U16/JV boys program in the country, school-based or club. All 50 states are welcome — you do not need to be a state select side to enter.

Is this sanctioned?

Registration runs through Rugby Xplorer. Sanctioning and insurance details will be published with registration.

What does it cost?

The team entry fee will be announced when registration opens. The expression of interest is free.

What is the format?

Ten a side across two days. Match length, pool structure, and guaranteed games will be published ahead of registration.

Where is it played?

Bozeman Sports Park in Bozeman, Montana. Pitch assignments, maps, and parking will be published ahead of the event.

Are there girls divisions?

Not in 2027. Submitting an expression of interest is still worth doing so we can gauge demand.

How do we find lodging?

Hotels, rates, and recommended stays in Bozeman will be published closer to the event.
