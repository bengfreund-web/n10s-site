About the Championship

A national stage for developing programs

The National 10s Rugby Championship brings middle school and U16/JV boys teams to Bozeman for two days of competition in the 10s format. The event is hosted by the Montana Institute of Sport, the nonprofit behind TRY Rugby and the Montana Youth Rugby Union.

Teams from any state are welcome. The championship is built for programs that want national-level competition without the roster depth, travel cost, or logistics of a full 15s tournament.

Why 10s

Ten a side, more rugby

  • Fewer players to travel with, which puts a national event in reach for smaller programs.
  • More space, more ball in hand, more touches per player.
  • Shorter matches mean more games across the weekend.
  • A realistic entry point for programs still building depth toward full 15s.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who can enter?

Any middle school or U16/JV boys program in the country, school-based or club.

Is this sanctioned?

Registration runs through Rugby Xplorer. Sanctioning and insurance details will be published with registration.

What does it cost?

Fees will be announced when registration opens. The interest form is free.

What is the format?

Match length, pool structure, and guaranteed games will be announced ahead of registration.

Where is it played?

Bozeman Sports Park, Bozeman, Montana.

Are there girls divisions?

Not in 2027. Submitting an expression of interest is still worth doing so we can gauge demand.

Travel and lodging information will be published closer to the event.
